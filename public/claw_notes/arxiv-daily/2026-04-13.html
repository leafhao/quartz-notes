1. RAG-Star: Enhancing Deliberative Reasoning with Retrieval Augmented Verification and Refinement

字段内容
作者Jinhao Jiang, Jiayi Chen, Junyi Li, Ruiyang Ren, Shijie Wang…
年份2024
arXiv ID2412.12881v1
PDF下载链接
分类cs.CL, cs.AI

摘要： Existing large language models (LLMs) show exceptional problem-solving capabilities but might struggle with complex reasoning tasks. Despite the successes of chain-of-thought and tree-based search methods, they mainly depend on the internal knowledge of LLMs to search over intermediate reasoning steps, limited to dealing with simple tasks involving fewer reasoning steps. In this paper, we propose RAG-Star, a novel RAG approach that integrates the retrieved information to guide the tree-based deliberative reasoning process that relies on the inherent knowledge of LLMs. By leveraging Monte Carlo Tree Search, RAG-Star iteratively plans intermediate sub-queries and answers for reasoning based on the LLM itself. To consolidate internal and external knowledge, we propose an retrieval-augmented verification that utilizes query- and answer-aware reward modeling to provide feedback for the inherent reasoning of LLMs.

3. RAG-Gym: Systematic Optimization of Language Agents for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

字段内容
作者Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Xiao Wang, Yin Fang, Haolin Liu…
年份2025
arXiv ID2502.13957v2
PDF下载链接
分类cs.CL, cs.AI

摘要： Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has shown great promise for knowledge-intensive tasks and recently advanced with agentic RAG, where language agents engage in multi-round interactions with external knowledge sources for adaptive information retrieval. However, existing agentic RAG methods often depend on ad-hoc prompt engineering and lack a unified optimization framework. We introduce RAG-Gym, a comprehensive platform that systematically explores three optimization dimensions: (1) prompt engineering, (2) actor tuning, and (3) critic training. For prompt engineering, we propose Re²Search, a novel agent incorporating reasoning reflection that significantly outperforms standard prompts.

4. FOCAL: A Novel Benchmarking Technique for Multi-modal Agents

年份2026
arXiv ID2601.07367v2
PDF下载链接
分类cs.SD

摘要： With the recent advancements in reasoning capabilities, tool calling using MCP servers and Audio Language Models (ALMs), development and integration of multi-modal agents (with voice and text support) has come to the industry forefront. We propose a framework, FOCAL to benchmark end-to-end reasoning, component-wise error propagation and error analysis for automated as well as human-assisted testing of multi-modal agents (voice to voice + text input). 6. TEAL: Tokenize and Embed ALL for Multi-modal Large Language Models

字段内容
作者Zhen Yang, Yingxue Zhang, Fandong Meng, Jie Zhou
年份2023
arXiv ID2311.04589v3
PDF下载链接
分类cs.CL, cs.AI

摘要： Despite Multi-modal Large Language Models (MM-LLMs) have made exciting strides recently, they are still struggling to efficiently model the interactions among multi-modal inputs and the generation in non-textual modalities. In this work, we propose TEAL (Tokenize and Embed ALl), an approach to treat the input from any modality as a token sequence and learn a joint embedding space for all modalities. With the joint embedding space, TEAL enables the frozen LLMs to perform both understanding and generation tasks involving non-textual modalities.
