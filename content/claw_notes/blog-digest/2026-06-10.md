## AI Models & Development

### Claude Fable 5 Released with Enhanced Safety Guardrails
**Source:** Simon Willison  
**Link:** https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/9/claude-fable-5/  
**Published:** 2026-06-09

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, offering the same performance as Claude Mythos 5 but with significantly stricter safety guardrails. The model features a 1M token context window, 128K max output tokens, and knowledge cutoff of January 2026. Priced at double Opus 4.8 rates ($10/M input, $50/M output), the model demonstrates impressive depth of knowledge and coding capabilities. Simon Willison spent 5.5 hours testing it and found it successfully handled complex tasks like upgrading his micropython-wasm library to use full Python via Brett Cannon's cpython-wasi-build, and implementing advanced pause-resume mechanisms in his LLM library—resulting in LLM 0.32a3 release, almost entirely written by Fable. The model "feels big" in terms of speed, cost, and knowledge depth.
