### 1. RAG-Star: Enhancing Deliberative Reasoning with Retrieval Augmented Verification and Refinement

| 字段 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **作者** | Jinhao Jiang, Jiayi Chen, Junyi Li, Ruiyang Ren, Shijie Wang... |
| **年份** | 2024 |
| **arXiv ID** | [2412.12881v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12881v1) |
| **PDF** | [下载链接](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.12881v1) |
| **分类** | cs.CL, cs.AI |

**摘要**：
Existing large language models (LLMs) show exceptional problem-solving capabilities but might struggle with complex reasoning tasks. Despite the successes of chain-of-thought and tree-based search methods, they mainly depend on the internal knowledge of LLMs to search over intermediate reasoning steps, limited to dealing with simple tasks involving fewer reasoning steps. In this paper, we propose **RAG-Star**, a novel RAG approach that integrates the retrieved information to guide the tree-based deliberative reasoning process that relies on the inherent knowledge of LLMs. By leveraging Monte Carlo Tree Search, RAG-Star iteratively plans intermediate sub-queries and answers for reasoning based on the LLM itself. To consolidate internal and external knowledge, we propose an retrieval-augmented verification that utilizes query- and answer-aware reward modeling to provide feedback for the inherent reasoning of LLMs.

### 3. RAG-Gym: Systematic Optimization of Language Agents for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

| 字段 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **作者** | Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Xiao Wang, Yin Fang, Haolin Liu... |
| **年份** | 2025 |
| **arXiv ID** | [2502.13957v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13957v2) |
| **PDF** | [下载链接](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.13957v2) |
| **分类** | cs.CL, cs.AI |

**摘要**：
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has shown great promise for knowledge-intensive tasks and recently advanced with agentic RAG, where language agents engage in multi-round interactions with external knowledge sources for adaptive information retrieval. However, existing agentic RAG methods often depend on ad-hoc prompt engineering and lack a unified optimization framework. We introduce RAG-Gym, a comprehensive platform that systematically explores three optimization dimensions: (1) prompt engineering, (2) actor tuning, and (3) critic training. For prompt engineering, we propose Re²Search, a novel agent incorporating reasoning reflection that significantly outperforms standard prompts.

| **PDF** | [下载链接](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.04589v3) |
| **分类** | cs.CL, cs.AI |

**摘要**：
Despite Multi-modal Large Language Models (MM-LLMs) have made exciting strides recently, they are still struggling to efficiently model the interactions among multi-modal inputs and the generation in non-textual modalities. In this work, we propose TEAL (Tokenize and Embed ALl), an approach to treat the input from any modality as a token sequence and learn a joint embedding space for all modalities. With the joint embedding space, TEAL enables the frozen LLMs to perform both understanding and generation tasks involving non-textual modalities.
