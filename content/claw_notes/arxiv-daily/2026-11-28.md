- **标签**：#线性注意力 #新架构
- **作者**：Xichong Zhang et al.
- **关键词**：HF 未返回
- **摘要**：Linear attention offers an efficient solution for long-sequence modeling via linear complexity and fixed-size recurrent states. Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) introduces independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing, yet it suffers from training failures during long-context training. We trace these failures to the asymmetry between reading and modification directions during erasure, which yields non-normal erase operators whose directional gains accumulate across tokens, triggering exponential state growth even without new writes. We propose symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state updates.
