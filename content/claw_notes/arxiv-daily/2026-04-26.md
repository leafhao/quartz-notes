### 1. [From Research Question to Scientific Workflow: Leveraging Agentic AI for Science Automation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21910)

**作者**: Bartosz Balis, Michal Orzechowski, Piotr Kica, Michal Dygas, Michal Kuszewski

**发布日期**: 2026-04-23

**摘要**: Scientific workflow systems automate execution -- scheduling, fault tolerance, resource management -- but not the semantic translation that precedes it. Scientists still manually convert research questions into workflow specifications, a task requiring both domain knowledge and infrastructure expertise. We propose an agentic architecture that closes this gap through three layers: an LLM interprets natural language into structured intents (semantic layer); validated generators produce reproducible workflow DAGs (deterministic layer); and domain experts author 'Skills': markdown documents encoding vocabulary mappings, parameter constraints, and optimization strategies (knowledge layer). This decomposition confines LLM non-determinism to intent extraction: identical intents always yield identical workflows.

**亮点**: 
- 提出三层架构解决科学工作流自动化问题
- 通过 Skills 文档将领域知识编码
- 在 1000 Genomes 工作流上验证，LLM 开销低于 15 秒，成本低于 $0.001/查询

**关键词**: `Agentic AI` `Scientific Workflow` `LLM Application`

### 2. [Tool Attention Is All You Need: Dynamic Tool Gating and Lazy Schema Loading for Eliminating the MCP/Tools Tax in Scalable Agentic Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21816)

**作者**: Anuj Sadani, Deepak Kumar

**发布日期**: 2026-04-23

**摘要**: The Model Context Protocol (MCP) has become a common interface for connecting large language model (LLM) agents to external tools, but its reliance on stateless, eager schema injection imposes a hidden per-turn overhead the MCP Tax or Tools Tax. We introduce Tool Attention, a middleware-layer mechanism that generalizes the Attention paradigm from self-attention over tokens to gated attention over tools.

**亮点**:
- 提出 Tool Attention 解决 MCP 协议的 token 开销问题
- 通过 Intent Schema Overlap 和延迟加载减少 token 使用
- 在 120 工具模拟中，将 per-turn tool tokens 从 47.3k 减至 2.4k（降低 95%）

**关键词**: `MCP` `Tool Use` `Agent` `Attention`

### 4. [Alignment has a Fantasia Problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21827)

**作者**: Nathanael Jo, Zoe De Simone, Mitchell Gordon, Ashia Wilson

**发布日期**: 2026-04-23 | ICLR 2026 Workshop HCAIR

**摘要**: Modern AI assistants are trained to follow instructions, implicitly assuming that users can clearly articulate their goals. Decades of behavioral research show that people often engage with AI systems before their goals are fully formed. When AI systems treat prompts as complete expressions of intent, they can appear useful but not necessarily aligned with users' needs. We call these failures Fantasia interactions.

**亮点**:
- 提出 "Fantasia interactions" 概念描述用户意图不完整时的对齐问题
- 强调 AI 应主动帮助用户形成和完善意图
- 跨机器学习、界面设计、行为科学的跨学科视角

**关键词**: `Alignment` `HCI` `User Intent`
